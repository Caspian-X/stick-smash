# Ninja, Dancer and Lancer kit tuning

This pass fits the existing original fighter kits to their intended roles. These are authored gameplay adjustments, not frame data imported from another game. Frame numbers below are zero-based simulation ticks; active endpoints are inclusive. No fighter-specific engine branches are needed.

Ninja keeps three 3% stars and their existing travel speed/range. Releases move from 7/12/17 to 7/16/25, and total commitment rises from 35 to 45 ticks. The 9-tick interval plus 0.55 hitstun multiplier gives a low-percent target a break between hits; 0.6 hitlag reduces repeated freezing. This is deliberately not a promise of escape at every percent or spacing. The final star still leaves 20 ticks of commitment, rather than making the slower volley safer to finish.

Dancer's Rising Spiral keeps its launch velocity and recovery height. Its autolink radius falls from 49 to 38, repeating hits from every 5 ticks to every 7, damage from 2 to 1.5, hitstun to 0.75 and hitlag to 0.7. The 4–18 window permits at most three contacts before hitlag (4/11/18). The finisher remains at elapsed 20–23, but deals 6 instead of 8, radius 24 instead of 29, and uses BKB 18/growth 0.9 in the existing reference-unit formula. Finish duration rises from 17 to 23 ticks, with 16 ticks of landing lag in both rising and finishing phases and helplessness on timeout. This reduces offensive coverage and launch power while preserving recovery travel. Spinning Top also narrows from radius 43 to 38 and repeats every 7 ticks, with 0.8 hitlag; its finisher stays intact.

Lancer's Measuring Thrust, Air Needle, Horizon Piercer and Coiled Thrust gain a radius-10 sweet spot on precisely their first active tick: 6, 6, 12 and 14 respectively. Their sweet damage is 17/18/25/22; BKB is the former tip's value plus 4, and growth plus 0.25. The remaining active ticks retain the former tip values and radius; shaft/body damage is unchanged. Centers remain at the authored spear endpoints, matching the fully extended pose and attachment keys. This follows Wizard's early sweet-spot principle while preserving Lancer's own reach and ordinary late tip. It rewards simultaneous spacing and timing rather than enlarging general melee coverage. Neutral-special charge still uses the shared charge system.

`crates/sim/tests/fighter_kits.rs` exercises actual release events and move completion, low-percent star hitstun against release spacing, every active/inactive boundary on the four Lancer thrusts, mirrored tip/shaft contacts through `combat::hit_pass`, stronger tip launch at percent, and Dancer's phase timeline, landing transitions and helpless ending. Focused kit, content-validation/canonical-format, and knockback suites pass. These are mechanical checks; live matchup playtesting remains necessary to assess overall balance.
